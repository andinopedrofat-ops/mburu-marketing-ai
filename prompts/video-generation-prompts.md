## INTRO / OPENER CLIPS

### V01 — Empty Gym At Dawn
**Platform**: Runway / Kling
```
Slow camera drift through an empty CrossFit gym at early morning,
single overhead LED lighting casting dramatic shadows across rubber flooring
and metal rigs, deep blue atmosphere, chalk dust visible in light beams,
cinematic 4K quality, smooth dolly movement forward, no people, 6 seconds,
atmospheric and powerful
```

## USAGE GUIDELINES

**Resolution**: Always request 1920×1080 (landscape) or 1080×1920 (vertical) output

**Duration**: Keep AI-generated clips to 4-12 seconds — they're most reliable in short bursts

**Integration**: AI video clips work best as:
- Establishing shots before real footage
- B-roll inserts during real footage
- Atmospheric intros/outros
- Placeholder during production planning

**NEVER**: Use AI video as the primary content featuring members or coaches.
Real people = real footage. AI video = atmosphere and concept only.

**Disclosure**: If AI-generated content is used in a post, ensure it's used as a supporting visual element, not as "evidence" of real events.
