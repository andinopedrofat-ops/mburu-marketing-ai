# STORIES SYSTEM — MBURUCUYA FITNESS CENTER
## Daily Content Architecture for Instagram Stories

---

## STORIES PHILOSOPHY

Stories are the brand's daily relationship layer.
Not every Story sells. Most Stories build trust, keep the audience warm,
and make the eventual CTA feel like advice from a friend, not an ad.

**Target**: 5-8 Story frames per day, posted in two windows:
- Morning window: 8:00–10:00am (catches commuters and early risers)
- Evening window: 7:00–9:00pm (highest engagement time in Paraguay)

---

## DAILY STORY ARCHITECTURE

### MORNING STORIES (8:00–10:00am)
```
FRAME 01 — ENERGY OPENER
Content: A real photo or short clip from today's class (or this week's classes)
Text overlay: Day + time reference ("Martes. 7am.")
Tone: Raw, real, energetic
Purpose: Show the brand is alive and active today

FRAME 02 — VALUE DELIVERY
Content: One quick tip, fact, or insight related to fitness/community
Format: Text on Deep Blue background OR illustrated fact card
Tone: Useful, brief, credible
Purpose: Earn attention before the evening ask

FRAME 03 — ENGAGEMENT
Content: Poll, question box, or quiz related to training habits
Example polls:
  "¿Ya entrenaste hoy?" → Sí 💪 / Hoy no pude
  "¿Qué te cuesta más evitar?" → Azúcar / Sedentarismo
  "¿Cuántos días por semana entrenás?" → 1-2 / 3-4 / 5+
Purpose: Generate data + keep audience interacting
```

### EVENING STORIES (7:00–9:00pm)
```
FRAME 04 — SOCIAL PROOF
Content: Member tag reshare / testimonial screenshot / result photo (with permission)
Text added: "Esto pasa en MBURU todos los días." or just the member's @
Purpose: Evidence that real people are getting results

FRAME 05 — SOFT CTA
Content: Deep Blue background, white text, yellow CTA element
Message: Gentle invitation — not hard sell
Example: "Si hoy se te pasó entrenar — mañana empezamos de nuevo. Link en bio para reservar tu primer clase gratis."
Purpose: Warm lead capture in the moment of most receptivity

FRAME 06 (Optional) — URGENCY/OFFER REMINDER
Only during active campaign weeks
Content: Countdown sticker + offer reminder
Purpose: Move warm audience toward conversion
```

## STORY DESIGN STANDARDS

### Text Overlays on Real Photos
- Use Deep Blue bar at 80% opacity behind text (never text directly on photo without contrast)
- Font: Inter Bold or equivalent — no Bebas Neue on Stories (too heavy for small text)
- Font size: Legible on a phone screen — test on mobile before posting

### Full-Screen Text Stories
- Background: Deep Blue solid
- Headline: Yellow or White, ultra-bold, centered
- Maximum 2-3 lines of text
- Include MBURU logo (small, bottom-right)

### Interactive Stories Rules
- Poll stickers: Always use brand colors in the poll design (Instagram allows color customization)
- Question stickers: Save all responses — they are content research and lead intelligence
- Link stickers: Use for all CTAs — set destination to WhatsApp contact or booking page
- Countdown stickers: Use only for real deadlines

## HIGHLIGHT ARCHITECTURE

Stories that perform well or contain evergreen content get saved to Highlights:

```
HIGHLIGHT 01 — RESULTADOS
Content: Member transformations, before/afters, testimonials
Icon: Trophy or star icon, Deep Blue background

HIGHLIGHT 02 — COMUNIDAD
Content: Group photos, celebrations, community moments, UGC
Icon: Heart or people icon, Yellow background

HIGHLIGHT 03 — CLASES Y HORARIOS
Content: Horarios posts, class type explanations, what to expect
Icon: Calendar icon, Deep Blue background

HIGHLIGHT 04 — PRECIOS
Content: Modalidades and pricing posts (keep current — update when prices change)
Icon: Label or tag icon, Yellow background

HIGHLIGHT 05 — PRIMERA VEZ
Content: "What to expect" guides, first-timer stories, beginner-friendly content
Icon: Door or star icon, Deep Blue background

HIGHLIGHT 06 — COACHES
Content: Coach profiles, Q&As, behind-the-scenes programming content
Icon: Whistle or person icon, Yellow background
```

**Highlight Cover Design**: Consistent style — all covers use the same template:
- Deep Blue or Yellow background
- Simple icon (white on blue, or blue on yellow)
- Text label below icon (short — 1-2 words max)
