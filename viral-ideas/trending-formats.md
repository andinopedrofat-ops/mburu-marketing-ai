# TRENDING FORMATS FOR MBURU — Content Format Playbook
## Formats That Work · Applied to Mburucuya

---

> **Rule**: Never chase a trend that doesn't serve the brand.
> Adapt trends to MBURU's voice — never adapt MBURU's voice to a trend.
> Authenticity > virality. But authentic + viral = the goal.

---

## FORMAT 01 — THE "POV" FORMAT

**What it is**: First-person perspective video. "POV: Es tu primer día en MBURU."

**Why it works for MBURU**: Eliminates the "I don't know what to expect" objection. The audience lives the experience before committing. Reduces fear of the first step.

**How to execute**:
- Camera at chest/eye level throughout
- Enter through the gym door (audience sees what a new member sees)
- Coach greets warmly (scripted but natural)
- Brief orientation → warmup → modified version of a movement → end on a high
- No voiceover needed. Text overlays at each stage: "POV: Tu primer día en MBURU"
- Caption: "¿Qué te pareció? ¿Venís?"

**Hook**: "POV: Por fin entraste." — Black text on white, first frame.

---

## FORMAT 02 — THE "MYTH vs. TRUTH" SPLIT SCREEN

**What it is**: Left side = the myth (person thinking/saying something wrong). Right side = the reality.

**Why it works for MBURU**: Directly tackles objections in a visually engaging way. High save rate (people save useful content). Shareable (people share content that validates their beliefs or corrects misconceptions in their social circle).

**Adaptation for MBURU**:
| LEFT (Mito) | RIGHT (Realidad MBURU) |
|---|---|
| "Tengo que estar en forma para ir al gym" | Principiantes absolutos bienvenidos |
| "El crossfit es para atletas" | 60-year-old member doing crosstraining |
| "No tengo tiempo" | Schedule from 5:50am to 8:00pm |
| "Es muy caro" | GS. 200,000/mes con todos los beneficios |

## FORMAT 03 — THE "DAY IN THE LIFE" (Rutina)

**What it is**: Follow a person through their day, with gym as the anchor point.

**MBURU Adaptation — "El día de alguien que entrena en MBURU"**:
- 5:50am: Box. Dark outside. Already moving.
- 7:00am: Shower. Different energy for the day.
- Work/daily life: More focused, less tired.
- Afternoon: Productivity connection to morning training.
- Evening: Rest, sleep better.
- Next morning: Back at 5:50am.

**Hook**: "El día empieza diferente cuando entrenás en MBURU."

**Why it converts**: Makes the gym feel like a life upgrade, not a chore.

---

## FORMAT 04 — THE TRANSFORMATION TIMELAPSE

**What it is**: 30–90 day member journey condensed into 30 seconds. Not body transformation — BEHAVIOR transformation.

**MBURU Adaptation**:
Week 1: Nervous. Modified movements. Checking the board.
Week 2: Familiar faces. Less modification needed.
Week 4: First unassisted pull-up moment.
Week 8: Helping a newer member.
Week 12: Celebrating a PR. Completely at home.

**What makes this different from generic before/after**: Focus is on BELONGING and COMPETENCE growth, not just body change. More shareable because it's about the journey, not appearance.

## FORMAT 05 — THE COMMUNITY CHALLENGE

**What it is**: Challenge that gets members filming themselves and tagging the brand.

**MBURU Challenge Concepts**:

**#DesafíoMBURU**: Film your workout, post with this hashtag, tag @mburucuyafitnesscenter.
Winner (most engagement): Free month of training.

**#MiPrimerWOD**: Film your first-ever WOD at MBURU. Post your reaction. Community encouragement floods in.

**#5K50am**: Complete 5 workouts in 30 days before 7am. Tag MBURU. The 5am crew grows.

**Why community challenges work**: Each participant who posts is advertising MBURU to their entire following. The brand's reach multiplies with every tag.

## FORMAT 06 — THE "COACH EXPLAINS" (Expert Authority)

**What it is**: Coach speaks directly to camera, explaining something technical in plain language.

**MBURU Adaptation**:
- "Por qué los burpees te hacen odiar la vida (y por qué los seguimos haciendo)"
- "La diferencia entre crosstraining y funcional — en 60 segundos"
- "Cómo escalar un movimiento si sos principiante absoluto"
- "Por qué el warm-up importa más que el WOD"

**HC MAMADO version**: Same format but with humor. HC MAMADO explains complex training concepts with deadpan comedy.

---

## FORMAT 07 — THE TESTIMONIAL SERIES (Elevated Version)

**Traditional testimonial**: Person says good things about gym. Looks staged.

**MBURU elevated version**:
- Film member before they know what they're saying (semi-candid)
- Ask: "What would you tell yourself one year ago?"
- That answer = the testimonial
- Frame it as a letter to their past self
- Black and white footage. Real tears or real laughs allowed.

**Why this version converts better**: "Letter to past self" format taps directly into the viewer's internal narrative. The viewer IS that past self. The testimonial becomes personal.
