## TIER 1 — HIGHEST VIRAL POTENTIAL

### V01 — "El primer día de [NOMBRE]"
**Status**: READY FOR PRODUCTION
**Viral mechanic**: Recognition + Open loop
**Format**: Reel (30–45s)
**Concept**: POV-style video following a new member's very first class at MBURU. No performance, no glamour — just the real experience. Hesitation at the door. The warmth of being received. The confusion, then the encouragement. The exhaustion. The smile at the end.
**Why it goes viral**: Every person who has ever wanted to join a gym but was scared will share this. It answers their biggest fear: "What actually happens when I walk in for the first time?"
**Hook**: "Este fue el primer turno de Valeria en MBURU. Ella no sabía qué esperar."
**CTA**: "Tu primera clase también es gratis. Link en bio."

---

### V02 — "Horarios que te dejan sin excusas"
**Status**: READY FOR PRODUCTION
**Viral mechanic**: Usefulness + Identity challenge
**Format**: Reel (15–20s)
**Concept**: Fast-cut montage. Each shot shows a different MBURU class time slot — from 5:50am to 8:00pm. Over each shot: a common excuse. "No tengo tiempo en la mañana." → 5:50am class. "Al mediodía no puedo." → 12:30 class. "En la tarde tengo reuniones." → 18:00 class. "Trabajo hasta tarde." → 20:00 class. Final frame: "¿Cuál es tu excusa ahora?"
**Why it goes viral**: Directly confronts the #1 objection. People tag friends. Defensive engagement (comments defending excuses) = more reach.
**Hook**: Text on black: "Me dijeron que no tenían tiempo para entrenar."
**CTA**: "Encontrá tu turno. Link en bio."

---

### V03 — "Acá nadie entrena solo" — Generaciones
**Status**: CONCEPT
**Viral mechanic**: Emotion + Identity Signal + Community
**Format**: Reel (30s)
**Concept**: A montage showing the AGE RANGE of MBURU members. Not staged — real footage. The oldest member lifting alongside the youngest. Different ages, different bodies, same energy, same community. Final shot: them high-fiving.
**Why it goes viral**: Challenges the idea that gyms are only for young people. Multiple audience segments see themselves. Sharing says "gyms can be for everyone."
**Hook**: "Tenemos alumnos de 18 a 70 años. Y todos levantan."
**CTA**: "Encontrá tu lugar. Primera clase gratis."

---

### V04 — "Lo que cambia antes de que cambie tu cuerpo"
**Status**: CONCEPT
**Viral mechanic**: Recognition + Save-worthy insight
**Format**: Carousel (8 slides)
**Concept**: Educational carousel about the invisible transformations that happen in the first 30-60 days of training — before any physical changes are visible. Sleep quality. Mental clarity. Energy levels. Discipline bleeding into other areas of life. Confidence walking into a room.
**Slide structure**:
- Slide 1: Hook — "Empezás el gym y esperás ver cambios en el espejo. El espejo es el último en cambiar."
- Slides 2-7: One invisible transformation per slide, illustrated with real member quote
- Slide 8: "Tu cuerpo es el trofeo. Pero estos son los primeros premios."
**Why it goes viral**: High save rate (people want to remember this). Shareable because it's genuinely useful AND emotionally resonant.

---

### V05 — The "5:50am Club"
**Status**: CONCEPT
**Viral mechanic**: Identity Signal + FOMO + Community
**Format**: Reel series (weekly)
**Concept**: A recurring weekly Reel format — every Monday, a 10-15 second clip from the 5:50am class. No script. Just the energy of people who showed up in the dark, before the city woke up. Over time, this becomes a ritual and identity marker.
**Why it goes viral**: Creates an aspirational in-group ("I want to be a 5:50am person"). Regular viewers start watching every week. Members who appear start sharing.
**Hook**: "5:50am. El mundo todavía duerme. Ellos no."
**CTA**: "¿El próximo lunes te sumás? Link en bio."

## TIER 2 — HIGH POTENTIAL, NEEDS DEVELOPMENT

### V06 — "No estás listo. Eso es mentira."
**Viral mechanic**: Counter-intuitive + Identity
**Format**: Reel (25s)
**Concept**: Address the myth "I'll start when I'm ready / when I've lost some weight first / when I feel better." Coach speaking directly to camera. No script — genuine, slightly confrontational, full of warmth.

### V07 — "HC MAMADO explica el WOD"
**Viral mechanic**: Humor + Community
**Format**: Reel (30s)
**Concept**: Coach (HC MAMADO character) explains an incredibly complex or brutal WOD with absolutely deadpan calm while the class looks terrified. Ends with: "¿Preguntas?" [everyone silent] "Bien. Tres, dos, uno..."

### V08 — "El momento en que todo cambia"
**Viral mechanic**: Emotion + Proof
**Format**: Reel (45s)
**Concept**: Multiple members, each filmed separately, describing the EXACT MOMENT they realized MBURU was different from any other gym they'd tried. Not the physical results — the feeling. Shot in black and white for gravitas.

### V09 — "Antes y ahora: No el cuerpo, la vida"
**Viral mechanic**: Recognition + Proof + Share
**Format**: Carousel (10 slides)
**Concept**: A twist on the before/after format. Left column: "Antes" — the invisible before. Right column: "Ahora" — the invisible after. Sleep quality. Social confidence. Energy with kids. Patience at work. Ends with one physical result as the last comparison.

### V10 — "Etiquetá a tu compañero de clase"
**Viral mechanic**: Community + Share + Tagging reach
**Format**: Static post
**Concept**: A visually powerful image (community group, or one of the identity word series) with the CTA "Etiquetá a la persona que te hace aparecer cuando no tenés ganas." Tagging = multiplication of reach.

## TIER 3 — EXPERIMENTAL IDEAS

### V11 — MBURU Behind the Programming
**Concept**: Series showing HOW a week of training is designed. The whiteboard, the coach thinking through it, why each element was chosen. Demystifies "the WOD" and builds massive authority.

### V12 — The Transformation Calendar
**Concept**: A member films 3 seconds every day for 90 days. MBURU compiles it into a Reel. Real-time transformation evidence.

### V13 — "Cosas que escuchamos en el box"
**Concept**: Humorous compilation of real (or recreated) quotes from class — mid-WOD thoughts, encouraging yells, post-WOD complaints. Relatable for members, intriguing for non-members.

### V14 — Coach Prediction Challenge
**Concept**: Coaches predict what a beginner member won't be able to do in month 1. Check in at month 3. The member proves them wrong (in the best possible way).
