## CAMPAIGN CONCEPT

**Campaign Name**: FORJADO LIBRE

**Core Idea**: Paraguay's independence wasn't given — it was forged. With courage, with community, with the decision to no longer accept less than they deserved. 215 years later, that same spirit lives in every person who walks through MBURU's door and decides to build a stronger version of themselves.

**Central Metaphor**: The founding of Paraguay = The decision to start training. Both require the same things: courage to begin, community to sustain it, and the stubbornness to keep going when it's hard.

**Campaign Line**: *"Paraguay se forjó con fuerza. Vos también."*

**Secondary Lines**:
- *"215 años de independencia. ¿Cuándo empieza la tuya?"*
- *"El mismo coraje que fundó una nación. Todos los días, en el box."*
- *"Libre de excusas. Fuerte de verdad."*

## TARGET AUDIENCE

**Primary**: Paraguayans 22–45 who feel national pride and are "almost ready" to start training. The Independence Day angle activates identity before the fitness message lands.

**Secondary**: Existing MBURU members — content celebrates their community and gives them something to share with pride (national pride + fitness community pride combined).

**Emotional State during Independence Week**: Reflective + Proud + Inspired. People are thinking about what it means to be Paraguayan, about legacy, about strength. Channel that energy directly.

## COLOR SYSTEM FOR THIS CAMPAIGN

**Base**: Deep Blue #0A1628 (brand primary — maintains brand identity)
**Primary accent**: Energetic Yellow #FFD600 (brand primary — unchanged)
**Campaign accent 1**: Paraguay Red #D52B1E (flag color — used sparingly)
**Campaign accent 2**: Pure White #FFFFFF (flag color — already in brand)

**Application rule**: Paraguay flag colors (red + white + blue) appear as accents ONLY. Deep Blue and Yellow remain dominant. Never compromise brand identity for seasonal color.

**Acceptable uses of red**: Border elements, thin accent lines, "1811–2026" text in red, the Paraguayan coat of arms as a graphic element.
