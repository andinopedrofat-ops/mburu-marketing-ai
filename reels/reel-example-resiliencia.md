## VISUAL DIRECTION

**Color Grade**: Community Mode — warm tones, lifted shadows, but with one high-contrast moment at the emotional peak
**Lighting**: Existing gym lighting + slight warm grade in post. Do NOT add artificial dramatic lighting — authenticity is the point.
**Camera**: Phone (preferred for this series — feels personal, not produced)
**Shot Types**: 70% close-up / 30% medium
**Transitions**: Simple cuts. No fancy transitions — they break the emotional intimacy.
**Text Style**: Ultra-bold condensed for the one-word title. Lighter italic for the subtext quote.
