## DESIGN PRODUCTION NOTES

**Tools**: Canva Pro / Adobe Express / Photoshop / Figma

**Font files needed**: Bebas Neue (free on Google Fonts) + Inter Bold

**Template approach**: Create Flyer 01 as the master template.
Flyers 02 and 03 are modifications of the same base — change headline and offer details only.
Maintain all other design elements for visual campaign consistency.

**Export formats**:
- Instagram Stories: 1080×1920px, PNG, under 30MB
- Instagram Feed: 1080×1350px (4:5), JPG at 80% quality
- WhatsApp image: 1080×1080px, JPG at 70% quality
