# HOOK RULES — MBURUCUYA FITNESS CENTER
## Non-Negotiable Standards for Every Opening Frame

---

## THE HOOK LAWS

### LAW 1 — THE 3-SECOND VERDICT
The audience decides in 3 seconds. In those 3 seconds:
- The VISUAL must signal quality and relevance
- The AUDIO must capture attention (beat, voice, silence used intentionally)
- The TEXT must create curiosity, recognition, or challenge

All three must work together. One weak element kills the hook.

### LAW 2 — ONE EMOTION, ONE SUBJECT
Every hook activates exactly one emotion and focuses on exactly one subject.
- Wrong: "¿Cansado de no tener tiempo y de no ver resultados?"
- Right: "¿Cuántos lunes más vas a desperdiciar?"

### LAW 3 — LEAD WITH TENSION, NOT SOLUTION
The hook is NEVER the answer. It creates the question.
The hook ends where curiosity begins.

### LAW 4 — 8 WORDS MAXIMUM (text overlays)
The brain under scroll-speed cannot process more than 8 words.
Ultra-bold. High contrast. Center frame.
Yellow on blue or white on dark. Never the reverse for hooks.

### LAW 5 — NEVER OPEN WITH THE BRAND
The first 2 seconds are not for the logo or the brand name.
They are for the HUMAN TRUTH that earns the right to introduce the brand.
Logo appears at the END. Always.

### LAW 6 — A/B EVERY HOOK
Shoot 2-3 versions of the opening sequence for every Reel.
Post the same Reel with different hooks in different time slots.
Data decides which hook wins. Intuition informs. Data confirms.

### LAW 7 — THE SCROLL-STOP AUDIT
Before locking a hook, ask:
"Would I stop scrolling for this if I didn't know MBURU?"
If the answer is "probably not" — rebuild the hook.

## HOOK CONSTRUCTION FORMULA

```
[PATTERN INTERRUPT] + [NAMED TENSION] + [OPEN QUESTION]

Example:
"No tenés tiempo." ← Pattern interrupt (common truth stated bluntly)
(pause)
"¿O no tenés prioridades?" ← Named tension + open question
```

---

## WHAT DESTROYS HOOKS

- Starting with "¡Hola a todos!" → Deleted
- Opening with the gym logo → Deleted
- First 2 seconds = explaining what the video is about → Deleted
- Text that takes 5+ seconds to read → Deleted
- Generic fitness imagery with no narrative tension → Deleted
- Upbeat generic music with no emotional arc → Deleted

## MUSIC-HOOK ALIGNMENT

| Hook Category | Music Direction | Energy Entry |
|---|---|---|
| Counter-intuitive | Silence → single note → build | 0s silence, beat at 3s |
| Confrontation | Lo-fi, understated | Matches reflective tone |
| Open loop | Cinematic underscore | Builds with the narrative |
| Identity activation | Hard beat drop at 2s | Immediate energy |
| Cinematic scene | Ambient → driving | Slow start, peak at reveal |
| One-word identity | Single sustained note | Held under the word |
