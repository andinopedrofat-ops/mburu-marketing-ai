### Secondary Palette

```
CHARCOAL      #1C1C1E    — Dark backgrounds, premium text
STEEL GRAY    #8A8F98    — Secondary text, dividers
GOLD          #C9A84C    — Accent elements (badges, decorative — as seen in brand)
WARM WHITE    #F5F0E8    — Soft light backgrounds for testimonial frames
```

### Color Rules

1. **Deep Blue is always dominant** — minimum 50% of any visual composition
2. **Yellow creates the action point** — CTAs, key words, highlights only
3. **White provides breathing room** — text on blue, clean moments
4. **Gold is decorative** — badges, borders, seasonal accents (not structural)
5. **Never use gradients on brand colors** — keep them solid and bold

### Psychological Application

| Scenario | Color Combination | Psychological Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Authority content | Blue bg + White text | Trust, credibility |
| CTA buttons | Yellow bg + Blue text | Action, urgency |
| Testimonial frames | White bg + Charcoal text | Honesty, humanity |
| Pricing displays | Blue bg + Yellow badge + White price | Value perception |
| Hook overlays | Black bg → White text → Cut to Blue | Weight, then energy |

## TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

### Confirmed Brand Usage (from observed assets)

The brand already uses ultra-bold condensed typography for one-word headlines.
This is the correct approach. Maintain and systematize it.

### Type Hierarchy

**T1 — CAMPAIGN HEADLINE**
- Font: Bebas Neue / Impact / equivalent ultra-bold condensed
- Case: ALL CAPS
- Size: 80–120pt (fills the visual space intentionally)
- Color: White or Yellow
- Usage: ONE-WORD IDENTITY series, campaign titles, hook text overlays
- Example: RESILIENCIA / DISCIPLINA / FUERZA REAL

**T2 — SECTION HEADLINE**
- Font: Bold sans-serif (Inter Bold / Montserrat Bold)
- Case: ALL CAPS or Title Case
- Size: 40–60pt
- Color: Yellow (on blue bg) or Charcoal (on white bg)
- Usage: Flyer headlines, carousel slide titles

**T3 — SUBTEXT / QUOTE**
- Font: Regular or Medium sans-serif
- Case: Sentence case
- Size: 18–28pt
- Color: White or Steel Gray
- Usage: Quote subtexts (*"Venir cansada... y aun así aparecer."*)

**T4 — BODY / CAPTION**
- Font: Regular sans-serif
- Case: Sentence case
- Size: 14–18pt
- Color: White (on dark) or Charcoal (on light)
- Usage: Caption body text, informational slides

**T5 — SCRIPT ACCENT**
- Font: Handwritten/script (sparingly)
- Usage: Member names in testimonials, warmth moments, seasonal ("Mamá" in script in the observed community post)
- Maximum: 1 instance per piece

## LOGO USAGE

### Logo Description
Collegiate arch badge shape:
- "MBURUCUYA" in large athletic lettering across the arch
- "FITNESS CENTER" in smaller text below
- Color version: Blue + Silver/White with light blue accent bars
- The logo conveys: athletic heritage, solidity, community institution

### Logo Placement Rules

| Context | Size | Position |
|---|---|---|
| Reel end card | Large (40% of frame) | Center on Deep Blue field |
| Carousel slides | Small (10-15% of frame) | Bottom-right corner |
| Flyers/static posts | Medium (20-25% of frame) | Top-left or Bottom-center |
| Stories | Small logo chip | Top or bottom area |

### Logo on Color Backgrounds
- On Deep Blue: White logo version
- On White/Light: Blue logo version
- On Yellow: Never (insufficient contrast)
- Minimum clear space: Height of the "M" on all sides

## PHOTOGRAPHY STANDARDS

### Style Definition
**NOT**: Stock photography, studio perfection, aspirational fantasy
**YES**: Real members, real gym, real sweat, real emotion

### Shot Categories

**PORTRAIT — THE INDIVIDUAL**
- Subject: One real member, mid-workout or post-WOD
- Composition: Head to waist, slight low angle (empowering perspective)
- Focus: Sharp on face/hands, background softly blurred
- Expression: Authentic — effort, focus, pride, or genuine smile. Never posed.
- Lighting: Dramatic side or overhead. Not flat. Not ring light.

**COMMUNITY — THE COLLECTIVE**
- Subject: Group of 3+ members, candid moment
- Composition: Wide to medium. Include the gym environment.
- Expression: Celebration, post-workout, connection. Never lineup poses.
- Lighting: Available gym light, warmed slightly in post

**ACTION — THE MOVEMENT**
- Subject: Movement in progress — lift, pull-up, sprint, burpee
- Technique: 1/1000s freeze OR intentional slow-motion for Reels
- Composition: Diagonal framing, dynamic angle (low or high, never eye-level)
- Detail shots: Chalk-covered hands, gripping bar, feet on box

**ATMOSPHERE — THE PLACE**
- Subject: The gym itself — without people, or people secondary
- Purpose: Brand world-building, FOMO generation
- Lighting: Dramatic. Deep blue gym LED lighting. Hard shadows.
- Include: MBURUCUYA wall signage in background when possible

### Color Grading Presets

**POWER MODE**: Crushed blacks, boosted yellows and golds, high contrast.
*"This feels like a championship."*

**TRANSFORMATION MODE**: "Before" = desaturated, cool. "After" = vivid, warm, full color.
*"Change is visible."*

**COMMUNITY MODE**: Warm golden tones, lifted shadows, soft highlights.
*"This is where you belong."*

**RAW MODE**: Minimal grade. Slight film grain. True colors.
*"This is real."*

## MOTION DESIGN GUIDELINES

### Reel Typography Animation
- T1 headlines: SLAM IN (no ease, instant appearance) or FADE IN over 0.5s
- Quote subtexts: Gentle fade in after headline lands (0.3s delay)
- CTAs: Slide up from bottom, yellow element pulses once

### Music Philosophy
- Music is not background — it IS part of the content
- The emotional journey of the music must mirror the emotional arc of the piece
- Beat drops, key changes, and silences are used as structural tools

### Transition Language
- **Hard cut**: For impact, intensity, contrast moments
- **Slow motion**: For emotional peaks, effort moments, post-WOD
- **Speed ramp**: Fast → slow at the emotional peak
- **No cross-dissolves**: Too soft for this brand
- **No Instagram filters**: Color grade is custom, never algorithmic
