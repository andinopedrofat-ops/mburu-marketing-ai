## THE BRAND IN ONE SENTENCE

Mburucuya Fitness Center (MBURU) is the community gym in Paraguay where real people — not fitness models — transform their bodies and their relationship with themselves, through CrossFit-inspired training that is intense, accessible, and built on genuine belonging.

## TARGET AUDIENCE PROFILES

### PRIMARY — "La que quiere volver a sentirse bien"
**Demographics**: Woman, 28–45, Paraguay
**Psychographics**: Has tried fitness before. Has kids and/or demanding schedule. Feels like she "lost herself" in daily obligations. Wants to feel strong, not just thin.
**Fear**: Starting over, feeling embarrassed, not belonging.
**Dream**: Being the mom her kids see as strong. Sleeping better. Liking her reflection.
**Content that converts**: Las Mamás MBURU content, RESILIENCIA/DISCIPLINA series, community group photos

### SECONDARY — "El que ya sabe que tiene que arrancar"
**Demographics**: Man, 22–38, Paraguay
**Psychographics**: Knows he needs to exercise. Has started and stopped. Makes plans that collapse. Self-aware about his inaction.
**Fear**: Being the weakest one there. Looking stupid. Not keeping up.
**Dream**: Feeling physically capable, earning respect, having the energy he had at 20.
**Content that converts**: Monday warrior content, myth-busting (beginner-friendly), member transformation stories

### TERTIARY — "El atleta que quiere más"
**Demographics**: 18–30, any gender, already active
**Psychographics**: Wants a community, wants programming, wants to improve specific lifts or times.
**Fear**: Stagnation, training alone.
**Dream**: PRs, competition, belonging to a team.
**Content that converts**: WOD content, athlete spotlights, performance milestones (PR boards)
