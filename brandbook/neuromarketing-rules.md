## THE 6 CONVERSION LEVERS

### LEVER 01 — IDENTITY SHIFT (Most Powerful)

**The Science**: People do not buy products. They buy versions of themselves.
The brain evaluates purchases by asking: "Is this who I am or who I want to be?"

**Application in MBURU content**:
- Never sell the gym. Sell who they become.
- Never say "join our gym." Say "become the person who shows up for themselves."
- The product is transformation. The gym is just the location where it happens.

**Examples**:
- Weak: "Unirse a MBURU tiene muchos beneficios."
- Strong: "Acá no venís a hacer ejercicio. Venís a convertirte en alguien que se respeta."

---

### LEVER 02 — LOSS AVERSION (2.5x More Powerful Than Gain)

**The Science**: Losses feel 2.5x more painful than equivalent gains feel good.
"You could lose 10kg" is weaker than "You're losing time, energy, confidence every day you wait."

**Application in MBURU content**:
- Make the cost of inaction vivid and specific (without shaming)
- Frame delay as active loss, not passive waiting
- Use present tense: "Every week you wait is another week without..."

**Examples**:
- Weak: "Vas a ganar energía si entrenás."
- Strong: "¿Cuántos lunes más vas a perder prometiéndote que el próximo es el definitivo?"

**WARNING**: Loss aversion must be used with empathy, not fear.
If content feels like an attack, it backfires. Make them feel *understood* first.

---

### LEVER 03 — MIRROR EFFECT (Recognition Before Persuasion)

**The Science**: The brain cannot be persuaded when it doesn't feel recognized.
Recognition activates the reward system. It signals safety. Safety enables openness to change.

**Application in MBURU content**:
- Describe their life, their excuses, their inner voice with surgical precision
- The more specific the description, the stronger the recognition
- Recognition MUST come before any brand message

**The Mirror Test**: Read the first paragraph of any piece of content.
Does it describe the audience's life or MBURU's offer?
If it describes MBURU first — rewrite it. Always start with them.

---

### LEVER 04 — SOCIAL PROOF (Specificity = Credibility)

**The Science**: The brain uses other people's decisions as shortcuts for its own decisions.
But generic proof activates skepticism. Specific proof activates belief.

**The Specificity Ladder**:
```
WEAKEST: "Nuestros clientes están felices."
WEAK:    "Cientos de miembros ya transformaron sus vidas."
MEDIUM:  "Ana perdió 12kg en 4 meses."
STRONG:  "Ana llegó diciendo que nunca había podido bajar de peso. 
          Hace 4 meses, tenía 89kg. Hoy tiene 77. Duerme sin pastillas."
STRONGEST: Ana herself, on camera, saying it in her own words.
```

**Rule**: Always move the specificity of proof as far right on this ladder as possible.

---

### LEVER 05 — MICRO-COMMITMENT LADDER

**The Science**: Each small commitment makes the next one easier.
The brain needs to maintain internal consistency ("I do X, so I must be Y kind of person").

**The MBURU Commitment Ladder**:
```
Step 0: Sees content (no action required)
Step 1: Watches full Reel (passive commitment)
Step 2: Saves post (active micro-commitment — "this matters to me")
Step 3: Follows account (identity commitment — "I am this kind of person")
Step 4: Sends DM ("I'm interested enough to reach out")
Step 5: Books free class ("I'll invest one hour")
Step 6: Attends class ("I showed up")
Step 7: Enrolls ("I'm committing")
Step 8: Refers friend ("I'm proud of this")
```

**Content rule**: Each piece of content should advance the audience ONE step, not five.
Asking someone who just watched a Reel to enroll immediately is a commitment gap fail.
Asking them to save the post or follow the account is perfect.

---

### LEVER 06 — URGENCY + SCARCITY (Real Only)

**The Science**: Scarcity activates the fear of loss (Lever 02).
Urgency activates the decision-making system by removing the "I'll decide later" default.

**MBURU Urgency Rules**:
1. **Only real urgency** — fake deadlines destroy trust permanently
2. Specify the actual constraint: spots, date, price change
3. State it once clearly, then reference it in follow-up without hammering
4. Warm delivery: "I wanted to make sure you knew before it closed"

**Examples of real urgency**:
- "Quedan 4 lugares en el turno de las 7am para esta semana."
- "La promoción cierra el domingo — no la renovamos."
- "Solo tenemos cupo para 12 personas en la clase de prueba."

## DOPAMINE MECHANICS

Use these structural tools to keep audiences engaged:

### OPEN LOOPS
Start with an incomplete thought. The brain CANNOT rest until it has closure.
- "Hay algo que nadie te dice sobre empezar en el gym." → Must swipe/watch to resolve
- "Hace 6 meses, [Nombre] no podía subir escaleras. Hoy:" → Must see what happened

### PATTERN INTERRUPTION
The brain ignores the expected. Break the pattern to regain attention.
- Expected: Motivational music + person lifting + "¡Vos podés!"
- MBURU version: Silence + person sitting on couch + "Yo también pensaba eso."

### REWARD STRUCTURES
Deliver the payoff only after emotional investment is built.
- The result → after the struggle
- The answer → after the question has been felt
- The CTA → after the human truth has landed

### SPECIFICITY TRIGGERS
The brain's credibility centers activate on specific numbers, names, and details.
- "Lost weight" → vague → low credibility
- "Bajó 14kg en 5 meses" → specific → high credibility
- "Bajó 14kg en 5 meses y dice que por primera vez en 10 años duerme sin despertarse" → hyper-specific → maximum credibility

## THE 3-SECOND RULE

**The brain makes a content judgment in 3 seconds.**

In those 3 seconds, three things must work simultaneously:

| Element | What It Communicates | How to Win |
|---|---|---|
| VISUAL | Quality + relevance | Real people, high contrast, brand colors visible |
| AUDIO | Emotional register | Intentional — not default, not generic |
| TEXT | Hook + curiosity | Max 8 words, creates an open loop |

**If any one of these three fails in seconds 1-3, the content fails.**
Not recovers — fails. The audience is already gone.

## THE EMPATHY AUDIT

Before publishing any piece of content, apply this audit:

**Question 1**: Does this content make the audience feel *understood* before it asks them to do anything?

**Question 2**: Is the opening about THEM or about us?

**Question 3**: Would someone who has never tried a gym and is deeply self-conscious feel welcomed or judged by this content?

**Question 4**: Is there at least one specific, real human detail in this piece?

**If any answer is "no"** — the content is not ready.
Empathy is not a nice-to-have. It is the mechanism that makes persuasion possible.
